* **Title:** *Journey to the Centre of the Earth*[1]
* **Author:** Jules Verne[1][2]
* **Retold by:** Jane Cadwallader[1]
* **Illustrated by:** Gustavo Mazali[1]

---

### **Exact Book Text (Pages 3–27)**

#### **Page 3: Meet the Characters**

&gt; **Professor Otto Lidenbrock:** A famous and very enthusiastic scientist[2]. Nothing is too difficult or too dangerous for him and he is determined to get to the centre of the Earth[2]. **Axel:** An orphan, he lives with his uncle, Professor Lidenbrock[2]. He is afraid of the dangers they find under the ground but he stays with his uncle and helps him when he can[2]. **Hans:** A guide from Iceland[2]. He is very big and strong and is good at making things with his hands[2]. He doesn’t talk very much but he is kind and clever[2]. Hans is very important for the success of the journey[2].

---

#### **Pages 4–5: CHAPTER 1: Into the volcano**

&gt; I live with my uncle, the famous geologist Professor Lidenbrock[3]. Our journey to the centre of the Earth started one day when he was reading a very old book from Iceland[3]. Suddenly, a piece of paper fell out[3]. It was a letter with strange writing[3]. My uncle couldn’t read it but I could[3]. The words were in reverse order[3]. It was from a man who lived five hundred years ago[3]. His name was Arne Saknussemm[3]. It said, ‘Go down the volcano of Snaefells Yoculand and you will come to the centre of the Earth. I did it myself.’[3] ‘Pack our bags!’ said my uncle, ‘We’re going to Iceland.’[3] I thought, ‘Oh no! Down a volcano? I don’t like that idea!’[3]

#### **Pages 6–7**

&gt; But my uncle loves adventures and that is exactly what we did[4]. We found a guide called Hans and went to the Snaefells Yoculand volcano[4]. We started climbing down into the volcano[4]. It was very cold[4]. ‘I hope we don’t find anything!’ I thought[4]. But at the bottom there were three chimneys… and on one of them were the letters **A S**[4]. ‘I knew we were right! I knew this was the volcano! Arne Saknussemm was here!’ called my uncle[4]. ‘Hurry up Axel! We’re on our way to the centre of the Earth!’[4] I looked at Hans but he didn’t say anything[4].

---

#### **Pages 10–11: [Chapter 2: The Caves &amp; The Underground Sea]**

&gt; Sometimes the tunnel went into big caves with stalactites coming down from the ceiling and stalagmites coming up from the ground[5]. It was beautiful and there was a strange light[5]. I told Hans, ‘You can remember which is which because stalactites has a ‘C’ for ceiling and stalagmites has a ‘G’ for ground,’ but Hans just looked at me[5]. He didn’t say anything[5]. Finally, we came to a forest of tall trees and then to an underground sea[5]. We were in a huge cave, so huge that we couldn’t see the ceiling and there were clouds just like in the sky[5]. ‘We must go across the sea,’ said my uncle[5]. ‘Across it?’ I asked ‘But how?’[5] I was hoping we could go back but Hans said, ‘I’ll use wood from the trees in the forest to make a raft.’[5]

---

#### **Pages 12–13: CHAPTER 3: Across the sea**

&gt; We sailed on the underground sea for many days[6]. My uncle wasn’t happy[6]. ‘We’ve been on this sea for too long!’ he said[6]. I wasn’t happy either[6]. I was afraid[6]. I saw big things under the boat[6]. Then, suddenly, I saw two monsters coming out of the water[6]. ‘Look!’ shouted my uncle[6]. ‘That’s an Ichthyosaurus and a Plesiosaurus[6]. They are extinct on Earth but they are still living down here!’[6] I thought it was the end of us, but the two huge animals didn’t see us[6]. They were too busy fighting[6]. ‘Quick!’ said Hans[6]. ‘Let’s get away!’[6]

#### **Pages 14–15**

&gt; Then, there was a really terrible storm with the wind blowing, thunder thumping, lightening flashing, huge hailstones, and balls of fire hitting our raft[7]. I thought it was the end of us![7] But then, with a last and huge thump, we were on a beach[7]. Uncle looked at his compass and said, ‘Oh no! The storm has brought us back to the same beach!’[7] I felt terrible, but Hans just said, ‘The raft is broken. I’ll make another one.’[7]

#### **Pages 16–17**

&gt; While Hans was making the new raft, I went with my uncle to explore[8]. We walked through the forest and found huge mushrooms as tall as trees, and huge animals like elephants[8]. We found a knife from the 16th Century too[8]. ‘Look Axel. This is Arne Saknussemm’s knife. We are in the right place!’[8]

#### **Pages 18–19**

&gt; We walked back towards the beach[9]. Suddenly, we saw two huge rocks and between the rocks the mouth of a tunnel[9]. On one of the rocks there were the letters **A S**[9]. My uncle was excited![9] ‘Look, Axel! He was here! Arne Saknussemm was here! Run and get Hans. This is the way!’[9] So we went once more into a deep dark tunnel[9]. But very soon we stopped[9]. There were a lot of rocks and we couldn’t pass[9]. ‘Hans, the gunpowder!’ said my uncle[9]. ‘We need an explosion to go through these rocks and continue our journey to the centre of the Earth.’[9]

---

#### **Pages 20–21: CHAPTER 4: On the river**

&gt; We prepared everything and then I lit the gunpowder[10]. I ran to the raft where my uncle and Hans were waiting for me[10]. There was a huge explosion and rocks flew everywhere[10]. ‘Hurray!’ we all shouted[10]. ‘That’s the end of those rocks, isn’t it?’ laughed my uncle happily[10]. ‘Now we’ll be able to continue our journey!’[10]

#### **Pages 22–23**

&gt; But we weren’t happy for long![11] Something strange was happening[11]. After the explosion there was a huge hole in the rocks[11]. Our raft started to move quickly towards it[11]. Then, we went down, down, down[11]. We were on a dangerous river rushing down more and more quickly[11]. We all held on to each other and to the raft[11]. Now even my uncle was afraid![11]

#### **Pages 24–25**

&gt; Then, suddenly, we stopped[12]. We were at the bottom of a narrow chimney[12]. We weren’t there for long![12] Soon, we began to go up the chimney[12]. Faster and faster we went[12]. The water below was boiling and there were rocks, lava and fire too[12]. The heat and the noise were terrible![12] ‘Ha! Ha!’ said my uncle[12]. ‘Hurrah! The volcano’s erupting.’[12] ‘Erupting?’ I shouted loudly because of all the noise around us[12]. ‘Why are you happy about that?’[12] Then, suddenly, I saw why[12]. Our raft went flying out of the top of the volcano[12].

#### **Pages 26–27**

&gt; We ran quickly away from the lava and sat down on a hill[13]. We were safe![13] Imagine how happy I was to feel the sun on my face and smell the green grass and hear birds singing in the trees![13] We looked at each other in surprise and then we began to laugh[13]. ‘That was an exciting adventure, wasn’t it!’ said my uncle[13]. He wrote a book about our journey to the centre of the Earth and became even more famous[13]. He also went on more journeys[13]. But not me![13] From that day on I stayed at home!
